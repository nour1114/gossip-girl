💋 About Gossip Girl

Gossip Girl is a drama series about a group of wealthy teenagers living on the Upper East Side of New York City.

Secrets, friendships, relationships and scandals are constantly revealed by the mysterious Gossip Girl.

Nobody knows who Gossip Girl really is.

👑 Main Characters

Blair Waldorf

Queen Bee of the Upper East Side. Ambitious, stylish and confident.

Serena van der Woodsen

Charming, adventurous and always at the center of attention.

Chuck Bass

Mysterious, confident and known for his complicated relationships.

Nate Archibald

Charming and kind, but often caught between complicated relationships.
